About

What is discrimination?

Discrimination means treating people unfairly because of their skin color, religion, gender, appearance, disability or background.

01

Equal opportunities

Everyone deserves to be the same and have the same opportunities at school, work and in daily life.

02

Respect

When you respect and try to understand people you can reduce stereotypes and help people feel included.

03

Awareness

Education and conversations help people understand the consequences of racism.

Everyday Racism

Racism is often hidden in small comments and jokes.

Racism at school is rarely loud or obvious. Instead, it hides in stereotypes, assumptions and comments that people think are harmless.

“It was just a joke.”

But repeated comments can make students feel unsafe, excluded and emotionally exhausted.

1 in 3

Students experience discrimination during school.

67%

Online hate speech among teenagers increased.

Every day

Small comments can leave lasting emotional impact.

History

The history of racism

When we understand history it helps us understand the present.

16th Century

Slavery

European countries where forcing African people into slavery so their economy could become better.

1863

Abolition

Slavery was abolished, but racism still continued in society.

Today

Modern Racism

Racism still exists through stereotypes, exclusion and online hate.

💬

🧠

🤝
